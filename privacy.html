TabRoute

Privacy policy

Effective October 23, 2026 · applies to TabRoute for Chrome and TabRoute for VS Code

Everything stays on your computer. TabRoute has no account, no servers, no analytics and no ads. We never see, collect, sell or share your data.

What TabRoute handles

To make your browser tabs follow your VS Code project, TabRoute works with this information, only on your own device:

  • Your open tabs: the address (URL) and title of each tab in the Chrome window you're using, and its tab group's name, color and collapsed state.
  • Your VS Code project: the name and folder path of the project open in VS Code.
  • Your settings: whether the switch notice and auto-backup are on, and the sites you chose to keep open in every project ("Always open").

Where it's kept

Saved projects, backups and recently deleted projects are stored in Chrome's local extension storage on your computer. They're never uploaded or synced anywhere. If you use Download in the Backups view, Chrome saves a backup file where you choose; what happens to that file is up to you.

How the two extensions talk

TabRoute for VS Code runs a small server that listens only on 127.0.0.1, your own computer, never your network or the internet. TabRoute for Chrome connects to it to learn which project is open. Web pages can't connect to it, and the messages contain only the project's name and folder path.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, transmit or store any of your data on our servers. We don't have any.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone, and we don't use it for anything other than making your tabs follow your project.
  • We don't load code from the internet; everything TabRoute runs ships inside the extension.

How long data is kept

Saved projects stay until you delete them. Automatic backups are kept for up to 4 weeks; recently deleted projects for 30 days. Uninstalling TabRoute for Chrome removes everything it stored. Uninstalling TabRoute for VS Code removes everything it stored.

Chrome Web Store user data policy

TabRoute's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

TabRoute is a developer tool and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If a change ever affected how your data is handled, we'd say so in the extension first.
